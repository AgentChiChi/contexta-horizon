## Start a new AI chat without starting your project over

**“Did we get approval, or did we just send the draft?”** A fresh chat can't answer that from memory. Horizon puts dated file, chat and mail titles together so you can see which record to reopen, or bring a few references into your next conversation.

### Open it before you connect anything

On a fresh install, click **Try a fictional example**. You'll see six Harborline references in the actual preview. Click **Exit example** and they're gone. Nothing is imported, and Copy stays off during the demo. To practice copying a reviewed handoff into ChatGPT or Claude, use the [fictional mailbox](https://github.com/AgentChiChi/contexta-horizon/releases/download/v0.5.2/sample-mail.mbox). For natural-language lookup inside Aside, use the [optional skill](https://github.com/AgentChiChi/contexta-horizon/blob/v0.5.2/Integrations/Aside/README.md).

### “Off” now means a new lookup doesn't open that feed

Select AI chats only, for example, and the reader opens the AI feed and collection manifest, not the dedicated file or mail feeds. Because imports may mix chat and mail, Horizon skips the imports folder unless all three required switches are on. Skipped data isn't reported as proof that no such work exists.

**Verified on synthetic data:** all 16 switch combinations; 81 switch-off transitions with cached and fresh results matching; 32 queries on the installed app with no demo leakage; 62/62 app tests and 18/18 static checks. These measure the app's behavior, not ChatGPT or Claude answer quality.

**Assets:** arm64 Mac application, optional Aside skill, fictional mailbox and SHA-256 checksums.
